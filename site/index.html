A / Learning flow

Four ways to prepare.

Lessons, category-based practice, timed mock exams, and hazard-perception study are organized as parts of one learning product.

B / Progress

Local first, by design.

Hive-backed local storage supports offline-first lesson, question, and progress data. Supabase handles authentication and user profile persistence.

C / Architecture

Clear building blocks.

Flutter and Material 3 power the interface. Riverpod manages app state, and GoRouter handles navigation.

↗ The repository describes the app as implemented with sample learning content, with further content expansion and backend hardening still on its roadmap.

02 / ApproachProduct thinking

Good tools make the next step clear.

Fast Menja is organized around the way people study: build knowledge with lessons, strengthen it through focused questions, then prepare with timed mock tests and hazard-perception practice.

The codebase separates screens, state, navigation, local storage, and remote profile data. That structure gives the project room to grow without losing sight of the learner’s task.
